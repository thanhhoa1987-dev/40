2. Pronunciation & Stress

Sound • Primary stress

3. Sentence transformation

Rewrite without changing meaning
  • Giữ nguyên nghĩa của câu gốc.

4. Present tenses

Present Simple • Present Continuous
  • often, usually, every ... → Present Simple.
  • now, at the moment, Look! → Present Continuous.
